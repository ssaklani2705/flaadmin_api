Yes Bank is hosting an exclusive seminar on Sustainable Finance and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) Practices. The event will highlight Yes Bank’s commitment to responsible banking, climate-conscious financing, and social impact initiatives. Senior executives, industry leaders, and ESG experts will share best practices on green lending, renewable energy financing, and sustainable growth opportunities.This knowledge-sharing session aims to inspire businesses, institutions, and individuals to adopt responsible financial practices and contribute to building a greener future.
