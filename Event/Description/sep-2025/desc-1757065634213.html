1. Introduction

  • The State Bank of India (SBI), being the country’s largest public sector bank, organizes a wide range of events, conferences, training programs, and webinars throughout the year.
  • These are aimed at customers, employees, corporate clients, startups, rural communities, students, and regulators.
  • The purpose is not only business growth, but also financial literacy, digital adoption, rural empowerment, and innovation promotion.
