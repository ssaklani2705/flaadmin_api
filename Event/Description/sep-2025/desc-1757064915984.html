1. Introduction

  • HDFC Bank organizes events, seminars, and webinars with the aim of promoting financial literacy, digital banking adoption, investment awareness, and professional growth.
  • These sessions are conducted in offline formats (customer meets, roadshows, branch-level events, campus engagements) as well as online formats (webinars, e-summits, digital campaigns).

2. Purpose of HDFC Events & Webinars

  • Customer Awareness: Educating customers about safe banking, fraud prevention, and new digital services.
