Punjab National Bank (PNB), one of India’s leading public sector banks, is proud to present its exclusive Customer Engagement and Financial Awareness Event aimed at empowering individuals, entrepreneurs, and businesses with knowledge of modern banking solutions. This event will showcase PNB’s innovative digital platforms, secure banking facilities, and customer-centric services that are shaping the future of finance. Attendees will gain insights into personal finance management, business credit opportunities, housing and education loan schemes, and investment strategies. Interactive sessions with financial experts will address queries related to digital transactions, cyber-security awareness, and effective financial planning. Special focus will be placed on PNB’s commitment to financial inclusion, rural banking, and sustainable growth. The event will also feature demonstrations of PNB ONE App, internet banking tools, and latest government-linked schemes.
